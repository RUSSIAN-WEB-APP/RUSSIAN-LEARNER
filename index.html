GENERATED PRACTICE
Add at least two words to start generating phrases.

The phrase engine deliberately uses conservative templates and your own saved vocabulary. It is practice material, not a claim that every generated sentence is the only natural Russian expression.

How it works

  1. It selects vocabulary you already saved.
  2. It favors items that need practice.
  3. It combines them using simple Russian sentence patterns.
  4. As your mastery grows, longer patterns become available.
